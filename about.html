About AstraMind Labs

An African AI-first technology company headquartered in Area 3, Lilongwe, Malawi — building intelligent software, cloud solutions, and digital infrastructure for global scale.

Leadership Team

AstraMind Labs is led by a team of technologists, academics, and engineers who combine professional depth with a shared commitment to building technology that creates lasting impact.

Stevens Prince Thengo

Chief Executive Officer (CEO) LUANAR • Open Distance & eLearning Coordinator

Stevens leads AstraMind Labs' corporate vision, strategy, governance, and external stakeholder relationships. He is responsible for long-term priorities, major organisational decisions, performance accountability, and ensuring the company remains aligned with its mission of building intelligent technology for African challenges.

In his professional career, Stevens serves as a Coordinator for Open Distance and eLearning at the Lilongwe University of Agriculture and Natural Resources (LUANAR), where he oversees remote and blended learning programmes. This deep operational experience in digital education infrastructure directly informs AstraMind Labs' development of Astra Academia.
